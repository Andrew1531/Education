name: School communication email
description: User's chosen email identity for communication with institutions.
---

Use the applicant's account email as the means of communication with schools.

**Why:** The user requested a workaround using the account email when told that server-sent school outreach needs a published backend.

**How to apply:** Preserve this account-email requirement when changing the school contact workflow. Do not represent opening a draft in a person's email service as an email sent or delivered by VerifED.

Mailbox ownership must not carry across deletion and recreation of an account, even when both the username and email string are reused.

**Why:** Registration does not prove email ownership, and a reused username does not establish continuity with the previous account holder.

**How to apply:** Bind mailbox proof to immutable account identity, remove it on account deletion, and require the replacement account to verify afresh. Never migrate old username-only proof onto whichever account currently uses that name.
