---
name: Uniform education guidance
description: Product direction favors one shared U.S.-context qualification workflow over country-specific registry integrations.
---

Keep the experience uniform across countries and focus on what users have earned and how it relates to the United States system.

**Why:** The user said, “I want to keep everything uniformed, and just want to take what they have earned and apply it to the United States system.”

**How to apply:** Prefer shared qualification inputs and U.S.-context guidance over separate registry workflows for each country. Preserve country and award differences when assessing evidence; a uniform interface is not permission to invent equivalencies.

Support entered education up to and including master's-level qualifications.

**Why:** The user said, “I want people to enter has high as a masters level accreditation”.

**How to apply:** Treat master's level as the requested upper educational scope, not an assertion of accreditation or U.S. equivalency. Do not automatically classify foreign professional titles or specialties as master's degrees.

Match users with U.S. institutions offering programs in the same field at the same level as their earned qualification, not the next study level.

**Why:** When asked which program levels matches should include, the user selected “Same level as earned qualification”.

**How to apply:** Keep academic-level and field matching separate from institutional prestige, admissions eligibility, and official credential equivalency. Explain uncertain foreign qualification levels rather than silently assigning a U.S. level.
